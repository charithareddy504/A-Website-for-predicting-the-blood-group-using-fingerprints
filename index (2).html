Model Information

This computational model utilizes Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for the identification of ABO and RH blood group antigens. Based on research by Patil and Ingle (2021) and Alshehri et al. (2018), the system identifies logical markers in biometric data to eliminate invasive sampling.
